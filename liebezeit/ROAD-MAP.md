## Where the hard work is

Bars 17 to 24 and 69 to 76 are the two drops and carry the heaviest curves on
the track. Bars 33 to 44 are the long groove and are deliberately almost
straight, which is where a player can go flat out and make time back. Bars 65
to 68 are dead straight and level on purpose, so Kalinka reads clearly in the
headlights with nothing else competing for attention. Bars 85 to 88 are the
Corkscrew: hard left at the crest, hard right into a steep drop, still falling
through 87, run-out at 88.

Net elevation over the whole track is a climb, which suits ending on the
outro above the landscape. Say if you would rather finish where you started.
